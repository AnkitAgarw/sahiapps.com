Days Until – How Many Days Until Diwali 2026?

Days Until counts the calendar days between today and any date you enter by subtracting the two dates, then shows the result on a colour-coded card. It also counts up from past dates (days since), useful for streaks or anniversaries, all offline and without ads clutter.

Worked example

Diwali 2026 falls on 8 November 2026. Counting from 13 September 2026, that is 56 days away.

To count days between two dates: subtract the earlier date from the later date. From 15 August 2026 (Independence Day) to 26 January 2027 (Republic Day) is 164 days.

How many days until Diwali 2026?

Diwali 2026 is on 8 November 2026. From 13 September 2026 that is 56 days away.

How do I count the days between two dates?

Subtract the earlier date from the later date; the difference in days is the count. For example, 15 August 2026 to 26 January 2027 is 164 days.

Does the days-until count include today?

No. The countdown counts the full days remaining between today and the target date, so the target date itself is day zero when it arrives.

How do I find how many days since a date?

Enter a past date and Days Until counts upward from it, showing how many days have passed – handy for streaks, sobriety counters or anniversaries.

Can I track multiple events at once?

Yes, add as many events as you like – birthdays, exams, trips or holidays – and they are sorted by what is coming next.
