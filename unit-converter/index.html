How many metres are in a kilometre?

1 kilometre = 1,000 metres. Metric length units all scale by powers of 10: 1 m = 100 cm = 1,000 mm.

What is the Celsius to Fahrenheit conversion formula?

F = C × 9/5 + 32. For example, 37°C converts to 98.6°F.

Is 1 GB equal to 1024 MB or 1000 MB?

Unit Converter uses the binary convention: 1 GB = 1,024 MB. Storage manufacturers typically use the decimal/SI convention instead, where 1 GB = 1,000 MB.

How many square feet are in an acre?

1 acre = 43,560 square feet, the standard measure used in Indian and international land transactions.

How do I convert km/h to m/s?

Divide the km/h value by 3.6. For example, 90 km/h ÷ 3.6 = 25 m/s.

How many millilitres are in a litre?

1 litre = 1,000 millilitres (ml).
