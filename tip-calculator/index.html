Tip Calculator – How Much Tip Should You Leave in India?

Tipping is not compulsory in India, but 5–10% is common at restaurants when service is good and no service charge is already included. Total = bill × (1 + tip%); per person = total ÷ number of people. Tip Calculator computes both instantly as you type.

How much tip should you leave in India, and is tipping expected?

Tipping is not compulsory in India. 5–10% is a common amount at restaurants for good service, unless a service charge is already included in the bill.

How do I split a bill with tip among 4 people?

Add the tip to the bill to get the total, then divide by the number of people. A ₹2,450 bill with a 10% tip totals ₹2,695; split 4 ways that is ₹673.75 each.

What is 10% of ₹2,450?

10% of ₹2,450 is ₹245.

What does "round up per person" mean?

It rounds each person's share up to the nearest rupee (or chosen denomination) so no one has to deal with paise, and the small difference is absorbed into the total.

What is the formula for tip and bill splitting?

Total = bill × (1 + tip%). Per person = total ÷ number of people.
