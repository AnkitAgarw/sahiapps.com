Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026. Applies to all apps published by Sahi Apps on Google Play, including our calculators, converters, trackers, Invoice Maker and Voice Recorder.

What we collect

Our apps do not require an account and do not send your entries to Sahi Apps. Information you enter—such as amounts, events, invoice/customer details, vehicle records, shifts, recordings and transcripts—is stored locally on your device and is deleted when you clear app data or uninstall the app. Content leaves the app only when you choose a system share action.

Advertising

Our apps show banner advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address and coarse location to show relevant ads and measure their performance, as described in Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from partner apps. You can opt out of ad personalisation in your device's Google settings.

Permissions

Our apps request the internet permission, which is needed to load advertisements. Voice Recorder also requests the microphone permission to record audio, and a foreground-service permission so a recording can continue while the screen is off. Recordings, transcripts and translations are created and stored only on your device; speech recognition runs on the device and nothing you record is uploaded to us. Optional language packs and translation models are downloaded from Google Play and Google ML Kit.

Children

Our apps are not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

Updates to this policy will be posted on this page with a new date.
